## The formal presymptomatic framework

| State | Immune markers | Metabolic findings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Susceptibility | May be absent | No diagnostic implication by itself |
| Single antibody | One confirmed islet autoantibody | Requires context and repeat assessment; not equivalent to Stage 1 |
| Stage 1 | Multiple confirmed islet autoantibodies | Normal glucose |
| Stage 2 | Multiple autoantibodies | Dysglycemia without overt diabetes |
| Stage 3 | Clinical diabetes, usually autoimmune evidence in this context | Diabetes-range hyperglycemia, with or without symptoms |

## Why time varies

Age, antibody characteristics, and metabolic change influence progression. The stages describe biology and facilitate monitoring; they are not a calendar that forecasts the exact day insulin will be required.
