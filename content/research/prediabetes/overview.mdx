## A category with several pathways

Prediabetes can be identified through HbA1c, impaired fasting glucose, or impaired glucose tolerance on an OGTT. These groups overlap but are not identical. The exact boundaries also depend on the guideline used. The [detailed prediabetes article](/type-2/prediabetes) shows ADA nonpregnant ranges. <Citation id="niddk-tests" />

## A useful opportunity, not a countdown

People may progress, remain stable or return below thresholds. Symptoms are often absent. Avoid interpreting one borderline value as a guaranteed trajectory; review repeat testing and context with a clinician.

## From a label to support

Evidence-based prevention emphasizes sustainable activity, nutrition, appropriate weight management and follow-up. The strongest trials enrolled specific high-risk groups, so not every effect size transfers to every person with an elevated HbA1c. <Citation id="ada-prevention" />
