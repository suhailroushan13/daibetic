## A controlled challenge

The nonpregnant diagnostic OGTT measures response to 75 grams of oral glucose, including a two-hour plasma value. Unlike an everyday meal, it standardizes the stimulus. Pregnancy has different protocols and criteria. <Citation id="niddk-tests" />

## Interpreting the two-hour value

Below 140 mg/dL (7.8 mmol/L) is below the impaired-glucose-tolerance range; 140–199 mg/dL (7.8–11.0) indicates impaired tolerance; at least 200 mg/dL (11.1) is diabetes range. Confirmatory testing usually applies in asymptomatic people.

## Why clinicians use it

It can reveal impaired handling that fasting tests miss. The tradeoff is inconvenience, preparation requirements, time, and biological variability. Illness, recent diet, activity and medications can affect interpretation. A consumer meal experiment is not an equivalent test. <Citation id="ada-diagnosis" />
