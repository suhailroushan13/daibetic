| Test | What it samples | Advantage | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HbA1c | Glycation of red-cell hemoglobin over recent months | No fasting; less short-term variability | Altered red-cell survival and hemoglobin variants; misses rapid onset |
| Fasting plasma glucose | One fasting glucose value | Accessible and inexpensive | Fasting and sample handling; daily variability |
| 75-g OGTT, 2 hours | Response to a standardized load | Detects impaired challenge response | Time, preparation and reproducibility |
| Random plasma glucose | Current glucose with clinical symptoms | Useful for urgent diagnosis | No defined prediabetes random range; context required |

## Disagreement has a reason

These tests measure different aspects of physiology. Confirm abnormalities according to clinical guidance, and investigate discordance rather than selecting the most reassuring result. <Citation id="ada-diagnosis" />
