| Feature | CGM | Fingerstick meter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sample | Interstitial-fluid estimate | Capillary blood estimate |
| Timing | Repeated readings and trends | A point-in-time measurement |
| Alerts | Available on many systems | Usually requires active testing |
| Limits | Lag, sensor pressure and device-specific interference | Technique, strip storage and meter accuracy |
| Clinical role | Management and pattern recognition in appropriate users | Management and confirmation according to device instructions |
| Diabetes diagnosis | Does not replace lab criteria | Does not replace lab criteria |

## Context matters more than gadget ranking

The useful choice depends on treatment, access, training and the question being asked. If symptoms disagree with a sensor, follow the device instructions and clinical plan.
