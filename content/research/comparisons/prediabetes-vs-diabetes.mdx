| Dimension | Prediabetes | Diabetes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Glucose category | Intermediate laboratory ranges | Diabetes criteria met and confirmed where required |
| Symptoms | Usually absent | May be absent or present |
| Trajectory | Regression, stability or progression | Course varies with type, biology and treatment |
| Main next step | Assess risk and prevention support | Classify, establish care and assess complications |
| Meaning of a threshold | Practical clinical boundary | Practical clinical boundary, not the first day biology changed |
