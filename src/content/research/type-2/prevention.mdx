## Risk can change

A higher-risk state is not a prediction of inevitable diabetes. Structured lifestyle programs have randomized evidence for reducing progression in selected high-risk populations. The opportunity is to reduce metabolic demand and improve health over time. <Citation id="dpp" />

## What happens inside the body?

Activity improves muscle glucose handling, while appropriate weight management can reduce insulin demand. Clinical teams may consider preventive medication for selected people. The choice depends on baseline risk, likely benefit, adverse effects and preferences. <Citation id="ada-prevention" />
