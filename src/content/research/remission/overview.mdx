## Define the outcome before celebrating it

Remission has a specific clinical meaning; “reversal” does not have an equivalent standardized definition. Normal glucose while taking treatment is valuable control. Neither statement establishes a permanent cure. <Citation id="remission-consensus" />

## The biological opportunity

Reducing metabolic stress can improve insulin action and, in some people, beta-cell function. Response differs and relapse can occur. The long-term DiRECT extension shows both sustained benefit in a subset and the challenge of maintenance. <Citation id="direct" />
