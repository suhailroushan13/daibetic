## Before symptoms

Compensation by surviving beta cells can keep glucose regulated during early autoimmunity. Feeling healthy therefore does not exclude presymptomatic Type 1. Symptoms generally reflect insufficient insulin and rising glucose rather than the first day of immune injury. <Citation id="symptoms" />

## When the picture changes

Glucose in urine pulls water with it, producing frequent urination and thirst. Energy losses and tissue breakdown can cause unexplained weight loss. Children may develop new bed-wetting. Fatigue and blurred vision can occur, but all these symptoms have other possible causes.

<Warning>Vomiting, abdominal pain, deep breathing, confusion or marked dehydration can signal DKA. Obtain urgent care rather than waiting for a routine appointment. <Citation id="dka" /></Warning>
